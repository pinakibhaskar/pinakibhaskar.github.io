Case study 1

Natural-language search in Samsung Gallery

Find a photo by describing it.

  • Vision-language model
  • Semantic image retrieval
  • Research →to product
Problem
People remember moments, not file names or tags. Keyword search cannot answer “the photo where we were all on the beach at sunset”.
My role
Team lead, architect and reporting manager. I coordinated three sites — Samsung Research in Seoul, the Mobile eXperience division in Suwon and our lab in Bangalore — to take the work from research to commercial release.
Approach
A vision-language model (VLM) for semantic image search in natural language, designed as a single unified backbone that also serves other Gallery features rather than one model per feature. We also built a machine-translation engine for Gallery search.
Outcome
Commercialised in Samsung’s Galaxy S25 series.
Recognition
  • Best Project award from Samsung Research, Seoul, for natural-language search using a VLM in Gallery on the Galaxy S25 series
  • New Valley Project of the Year (R&D), Samsung R&D Institute India – Bangalore (SRI-B) Annual Awards 2024
  • Super Tech Award (Development to Market), Q4 2024

Case study 2

On‑device personalised knowledge graph

Personal context, built and kept on the device.

  • Knowledge graph
  • On-device ML
  • Personalisation
  • Privacy
Problem
Personalised features need to understand a user’s own data, but that data is scattered across apps — and it is private.
My role
Led the team from research through commercialisation, in collaboration with Samsung’s Big Data Center.
Approach
A user-centric knowledge graph constructed on the phone from the phone’s own data. Published building blocks: on-device attribute extraction from unstructured text (ICON 2021), multi-hop relation-aware representations for inductive knowledge graphs (ICON 2023), and POP-VQA (WACV 2024) — an on‑device visual question answering system that personalises its answers with the user’s knowledge graph, improving accuracy by 36% absolute on KVQA 1‑hop questions.
Outcome
Commercialised starting with the Galaxy S21 series. A granted US patent (US11989193B2) covers rewriting search queries using the user’s context and personalised knowledge graph.
Recognition
Samsung-internal global Best Project award, Mobile Developer Conference 2021 (Mobile R&D Office, Korea).
Links

  • ICON 2023 paper
  • ICON 2021 paper
